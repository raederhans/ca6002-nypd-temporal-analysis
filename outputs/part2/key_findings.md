# Part 2 Key Findings

- The analysis contains **141,870 recorded arrests** from **1 Jan to 30 Jun 2026**.
- **Brooklyn** has the largest raw borough count (42,877), but this is not a population-adjusted rate.
- **Precinct 75** has the highest recorded volume (5,341); the top 10 precincts account for **27.1%** of records.
- The under-18 group has a **63.2% felony share**, the clearest age-severity contrast in the descriptive profiles.
- The largest borough departure is **Manhattan — Misdemeanor (+2.25 percentage points)** relative to the citywide share.
- Association strength is small: Cramér's V is **0.064** for borough × severity and **0.077** for age × severity.

These findings describe recorded arrest activity and should not be interpreted as crime incidence or causal effects.
